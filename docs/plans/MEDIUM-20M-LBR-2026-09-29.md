# Medium abstraction at 20M: new SB SOTA at the 20M budget, still loses to tiny-5M (2026-09-29)

## The measurement

The medium-20M LBR was never captured earlier today because the
criterion run hit the stdout/stderr interleaving bug (gotcha 5.8). The
`par-medium-20M-lbr-1k.log` was 0 bytes. Re-measured cleanly with
stdout/stderr split, 1000 deals, depth 100, seed 7 (the same seed used
by every other LBR in this table).

Two independent handoffs disagree on the tiny-5M number (15040/12050
mean 13545 vs 13977/12858 mean 13417). Both are 1000-deal LBRs. The
mean is within 128 mb/hand in either case; the medium result is above
both means.

## What the number says

- **New SB SOTA at the 20M budget.** 13 237 beats every other 20M-or-
  longer run on seat 0 (tiny 20M no-fix 13 319, warmfix 13 554,
  eps02 13 682, tiny 50M 13 572). The medium abstraction at 20M has
  strictly more infosets than tiny, so it has more capacity to sharpen
  SB, and SB's equilibrium is closer to pure — the extra capacity
  helps.

- **BB still collapsed.** 14 021 vs the tiny 5M peak 12 050. Medium
  does not fix the freeze: the RM+ current iterate freezes on BB just
  as tiny does, and the medium abstraction inherits it. The BB value
  is essentially the same as tiny 20M warmfix (14 090) and slightly
  better than tiny 20M no-fix (14 706), but nothing close to 5M.

- **Mean is basically tied with tiny 20M eps=0.02** (13 629 vs 13 667,
  a 38 mb/hand difference — well inside run-to-run noise at 1000
  deals). Both are well above the tiny 5M peak mean (13 417-13 545).

- **Matched visits/infoset:** medium at 20M is ~238 visits/infoset
  (84k infosets), tiny at 5M is ~238 visits/infoset (21k infosets).
  So at matched visits/infoset and ~3x the wall, medium does not beat
  tiny-5M. This is consistent with the full-9M finding: capacity alone
  does not beat the tiny-5M sweet spot.

## What it means

The medium abstraction is not the frontier. The frontier is still
tiny at 5M. Medium is a useful SB specialist at the 20M budget but
not competitive on mean because it inherits the RM+ freeze on BB.

This is exactly what `RM-PLUS-FREEZE-2026-09-29.md` predicts: the
freeze is a property of RM+ on a deterministic training loop, not of
the abstraction. Adding infosets does not change the freeze mechanism.

## What could change the conclusion

1. **medium 20M with the freeze fix** (eps=0.02 or DCFR alpha=0.9).
   Would separate "medium abstraction" from "medium + freeze" and
   would tell us whether medium becomes the frontier once the freeze
   is removed.

2. **medium 5M.** Same wall as tiny 5M. Would show whether medium is a
   better or worse per-wall choice at the peak budget. At 5M, medium
   would be ~60 visits/infoset vs tiny's 238 — likely undersampled.

3. **medium 50M.** Would tell us whether the medium mean curve has a
   higher peak than tiny's even if it starts worse.
