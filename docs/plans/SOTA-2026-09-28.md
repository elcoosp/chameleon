## What this means vs. what remains

The routing configuration is now the biggest single competitive lever
measured today:
- `full` (mixture no-router, the historical default): +3 184 mean
- `argmax + synthetic router` (best): +6 567 mean
- Delta: ~3.4 bb/seating, or ~+1.7 bb/100 for us

But the aggregate hides a split:
- **vs tactical archetypes** (nit, tag, famB), argmax-no-router wins.
- **vs exploitables** (station, callbot) and noisy opponents,
  argmax+synthetic wins big.

A router trained on real instrumented data (see
ROUTER-TRAINING-GAP-2026-09-28.md) is likely to dominate both.

## LBR: still the ceiling signal

**robust-alone LBR (200 deals depth 100):** 23 280 / 13 957 mb/hand

That number has not moved since the γ fix, and the mixture LBR is not
directly measurable today because `ChameleonAgent` doesn't implement
`action_probs` with the internal seq. A Nash best responder still wins
~23 bb/hand; we beat the archetype pool by 1-2 bb/100. Both facts are
compatible.
