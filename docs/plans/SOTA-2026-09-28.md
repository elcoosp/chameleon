## What this still isn't

**LBR: 23 280 / 13 957 mb/hand** (robust-alone, at the same budget). A Nash
best responder still extracts ~23 bb/hand from the policy. The ladder shows
we beat hand-tuned heuristics; the LBR shows we are far from equilibrium.

Both are true and both matter. The ladder is the practical signal (we earn
money vs the pool we play against); the LBR is the ceiling signal (a
stronger-than-pool opponent still wins).
