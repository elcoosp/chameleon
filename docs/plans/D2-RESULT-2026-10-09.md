# Decision D2 — RESULT (2026-10-09): FAIL, plateau confirmed

## The numbers

`train-pcs` on the tiny abstraction, 50 000 iterations (62 min wall,
well under the 4h budget; 81 354 rows, saturated by iteration 5 000).
Measured with the D1 full-game VBR harness, 180 boards, shuffled ranges:

| policy | VBR (bb/hand) | boards | miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| shipped blueprint (`agent-honest-19dim`) | 8.5196 +/- 0.6208 | 180 | 1.52% |
| **PCS DCFR, 50k iters** | **9.0977 +/- 0.6707** | 180 | 0.58% |

Difference: +0.58 bb (PCS slightly worse). Combined SE 0.91 → **z = 0.64,
not significant**. The two are statistically indistinguishable.

## The gate

Plan (line 113):

> **D2:** PCS at <= 4 h wall beats shipped bundle's VBR by > 3 SE *and*
> is monotone-decreasing in iterations.

- Wall: 62 min ≤ 4h. **Pass.**
- VBR < 8.52 - 3*0.62 = 6.66. **FAIL** (9.10 is above even 8.52).
- Monotone: not tested (gate already failed).

**D2 FAILS.**

## The pivot — the plan predicted this

Plan (line 113, kill rule):

> If PCS also plateaus at the same level ⇒ the floor is the *abstraction*,
> skip to W2 immediately (more buckets/real tree) before more trainer
> work.

**The numbers are exactly that plateau.** PCS 9.10 vs shipped 8.52, ~1σ
apart. Two independent trainers — external-sampling MCCFR and
public-chance-sampling DCFR — converge to the same ~8.5-9 bb
exploitability on the tiny abstraction. The trainer is not the bottleneck.

**Directive: skip to W2.** More buckets, real preflop tree,
abstraction v3. The plan says W2 before any more trainer work, and this
result is why.

## What else this settles

1. **C-2 (trainer defect vs abstraction floor).** The plan listed this
   as an open question. **Answer: abstraction floor.** Two different
   training algorithms, same number.
2. **The PCS trainer itself is not broken.** Its artifact loads with
   0.58% miss, its rows saturate as expected, its convergence is real.
   It simply converges to the same place the shipped trainer does.
3. **The speedup question is moot.** A 17x walk speedup would let PCS
   run 1M iterations instead of 50k. But the 81 354 rows saturated at
   5k; 50k already visits each ~10x over. More iterations would refine
   regret slowly (O(1/sqrt(T))) toward the same floor. No speedup is
   warranted until W2 gives the trainer more to work with.

## The 3-board smoke was a bad draw

Before the 180-board run, a 3-board smoke gave 2.46 +/- 1.49 bb. The
180-board mean is 9.10. With per-board std ~9 bb, a 3-board sample has
SE ~5.2 — the smoke was a 1.3σ draw. **Lesson: a 3-board smoke tests
plumbing, never magnitude.** It did its job (0.57% miss, artifact loads)
and its number should have been ignored.
