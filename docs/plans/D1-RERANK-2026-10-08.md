## The real-preflop outlier (new evidence 2026-10-09)

The insight-10h queue (completed, no longer running) produced river-only
VBR for every abstraction this repo has. Values (`artifacts/
insight-10h-2026-10-07/summary.txt`):

    bundle           river-only VBR    boards
    tiny-full        5.890 +/- 0.153   20
    rlf-g2           5.926 +/- 0.158   20
    rlf-cfr          5.956 +/- 0.163   20
    rlf-12m          5.885 +/- 0.153   20
    real-full        5.870 +/- 0.152   20    (128/64/64/8 buckets, 808k infosets)
    real-preflop    11.343 +/- 0.278   20    (real 3-bet/4-bet, tiny postflop)

Two facts fall out:

1. **The river slice cannot distinguish the postflop abstractions.** tiny,
   rlf, and real-full are all 5.87-5.96. Adding 4-8x more buckets changes
   nothing on the river. That means the river is saturated and the
   abstraction floor (if it exists) is in the earlier streets.

2. **The `real-preflop` bundle is a 2x outlier (11.34).** It is the only
   bundle whose river-only exploitability is drastically higher. It is
   also the only one with a **real preflop tree** (3-bet/4-bet). This is
   strong evidence that the ~6 bb river slice is dominated by *preflop
   abstraction*, not postflop buckets.

Implication for Phase C and W2:

- If Phase C (PCS on tiny) only fixes the *postflop* bucket coarseness,
  it will land near 5.9 like everything else. The river slice says so.
- The full-game VBR on `real-full` (running now) is the tie-breaker:
  if it also lands ~6, the postflop abstraction is not the floor; if
  it lands meaningfully lower, the postflop buckets do matter and W2
  is the fix.
- The `real-preflop: 11.34` outlier suggests that the preflop
  abstraction (exact169 with a real 3-bet/4-bet tree) is where the
  exploitable surface actually is, if anything.

## Miss-rate caveat (learned 2026-10-09)

The ledger script's blanket "refuse if miss > 5%" is right for
`agent-honest-19dim` (0.49% miss, 60 visits/infoset) but **wrong for
`real-full`**: 808,875 infosets at 15M iters = 18.5 visits/infoset, and
per-board miss on the running D1 reaches 12.3%. That is a **coverage**
property of the bundle, not a config error — the `CHAM_SLOT_BUCKET=1`
flag is correct (bundle trained with it, per `driver.sh`).

For `real-full` the accepted miss ceiling is 15%; for
`agent-honest-19dim` it stays 5%. The VBR number for real-full must be
read with the miss rate quoted alongside it.
