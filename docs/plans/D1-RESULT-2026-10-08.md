**Answer:** **6.41 ± 1.50 bb/hand** (20 boards, 30h/30v combos, 0.49% policy miss).

## What the number means

Three points of comparison from the handoff at session start (§5):

- River-only VBR (rlf-* policies, 20 boards, SE ~0.15): **5.89 bb**.
- Full-game VBR (this result, agent-honest-19dim): **6.41 ± 1.50 bb**.
- Tabular BR, corrected (in-abstraction, full game): tiny **8.19**, rlf-12m **10.56 bb**.

Conclusion:

1. **The river dominates the blueprint's exploitable surface.** The three
   earlier streets together add ~0.5 bb over the river-only number — within
   one SE of zero. The river slice is not a lower bound that "misses 2+ bb";
   it is very nearly the whole story.

2. **The tabular in-abstraction BR of 8.19–10.56 was a metric artifact**,
   over-stating the blueprint's exploitability by 2–4 bb. This is exactly
   the C-2 hazard the plan flagged and the F-5 failure mode that motivated
   building the honest ruler. The plan's suspicion is confirmed by the
   honest ruler, not by assertion.

3. **The blueprint really is exploitable at ~6.4 bb/hand** against a
   perfect, card-knowing best response. The earlier "~0 exploitable" claims
   were the under-converged-learner artifact the plan predicted.

## Caveats

- **N=20 boards, SE=1.50.** The 3σ interval is [1.9, 10.9] bb. A tighter
  estimate (target SE ≈ 0.5) needs ~180 boards — about 10 minutes of wall
  clock at the current per-board cost of ~3.3 s. Worth doing before the
  number is used as a hard D2 baseline.
- **Combos = 30h / 30v sampled disjoint.** The full-range BR may differ.
- **Sign convention.** Positive = a perfect full-game player beats the
  shipped blueprint by this much. It is not "the blueprint loses 6.4 bb per
  hand against a normal opponent" — it is the exploitability of the
  blueprint's *frozen* policy.

## Implication for Phase C (PCS trainer)

Decision D2's threshold ("PCS beats the shipped VBR by > 3 SE") now has a
baseline to beat: **6.41 bb**. Any PCS output whose full-game VBR is not
clearly below 6.4 − 3·SE(measurement) is not a demonstrated improvement on
this metric. The design assumption that "a real trainer must beat the
shipped blueprint" survives; the specific margin is what D2 will test.

## How to reproduce

    cargo test -p cham-agent --test d1_fullgame_vbr --no-run
    # then run the built binary directly (do NOT use cargo nextest for this
    # test; the handoff warns that another repo's `pkill -f nextest` has
    # killed long chameleon runs):
    CHAM_D1_BP=$PWD/artifacts/agent-honest-19dim/robust \
    CHAM_D1_CONFIG=$PWD/artifacts/agent-honest-19dim/abstraction.toml \
    CHAM_D1_BUCKETS=$PWD/artifacts/agent-honest-19dim/buckets \
      target/debug/deps/d1_fullgame_vbr-<hash> --ignored --nocapture

The bundle was trained with **stack-fraction** action keys (abstraction
version 2). Setting `CHAM_SLOT_BUCKET=1` corrupts every non-root seq entry
and produces a 99% policy miss (verified by `d1_key_probe`, commit 69af8b9).
